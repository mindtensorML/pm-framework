# Portfolio Construction Framework (v2.2)

Live map at https://mindtensorml.github.io/pm-framework/

A general portfolio construction process, from purpose and the investment policy statement through capital market expectations, methodology, construction and execution, to monitoring and adaptation. It routes by objective type and by selection method. Mapped against CFA Institute guidance on the portfolio management process and the elements of an IPS, with GIPS 2020 for performance and MiFID II for best execution.

- 7 steps and an illiquid deal subprocess, with 58 tasks, 10 decision gates and 3 external parameters.
- The board approves the mandate, the investment committee or client approves the IPS and the target portfolio, and binding commitments stay with people. Rebalancing runs within ranges written into the IPS.
- 12 tasks a model can run with the owner accountable, 41 a model drafts and the owner signs, and 5 stay with people. Deals get integrity, sanctions and conflict checks with recusals minuted, systematic models are validated independently, and a strategic-holding trigger goes to the board.

Built by Caesar Rana, CFA, APMG Certified PPP Professional (CP3P), at SpaceXAI and shared with permission. Revised in October 2026. The original map is archived in `v1/`. The map describes a general method and contains no client data. The tags are design estimates of what a frontier model could do with the owner accountable, not measured results.
